## Eleven Labs Settings
- Eleven Multilingual v2 (or Eleven English v1/v2 if available)
- Speed (Set to 1.0 / Default)
- Stability (Set to 65% – 75%)
- Similarity (Set to 75% – 85%)
- Style Exaggeration slider (0% or 10%)
- Chapter Headings: Ensure the section or chapter title is explicitly written at the beginning of the text block so the AI reads it aloud. ACX requires every file to begin with its respective header.
- Beginning Silence (Room Tone): Add exactly 0.5 to 1.0 seconds of blank space at the very start of each chapter block before the text begins.
- Ending Silence (Room Tone): Insert exactly 1.0 to 5.0 seconds of blank space at the very end of your chapter text block.

## ACX Audio Specifications
- Audio PropertyMandatory ACX Requirement
- File Format - MP3 only
- Bit Rate Type - Constant Bit Rate (CBR) (Variable Bit Rate is automatically rejected)
- Bit Rate Speed - 192 kbps or higher
- Sample Rate - 44.1 kHz
- Channel Mode - Mono or Stereo (Must be consistent across all files)
- RMS Volume - Between -23 dB and -18 dB RMS
- Peak Amplitude - Maximum -3 dB (To prevent clipping)
- Noise Floor - Maximum -60 dB

## How to Handle Amazon via INaudio
1. When Voices by INaudio delivers your audiobook to Audible and Amazon, the product page is created on Amazon. However, because the title comes through a third-party aggregator rather than your direct KDP account, Amazon's systems will not automatically link the audiobook format to your Amazon Author Central profile.
1. Once the audiobook goes live on Amazon/Audible:
    1. Log into your Amazon Author Central Account.  
    1. Click on the Books tab in the top navigation menu.  
    1. Click "+ Add a book". 
    1. Search for Supermarket Shenanigans by title or search specifically for the Audible ASIN/ISBN.
    1. Select the audiobook listing and click "This is my book".
1. Once approved by Amazon (usually takes 1 to 3 days):
    1. Amazon will automatically stack all available formats on a single sales page. Readers visiting your book page will see selectable buttons for Kindle, Paperback, and Audiobook.  
    1. The audiobook cover will appear on your official Amazon Author Page alongside your print and Kindle editions.
